## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-19

### Added

- Initial release of Universal POS Core.
- Domain-agnostic engine with customizable product metadata and units.
- Hardware scanner ready with low-latency barcode listener.
- Hexagonal Architecture (Ports & Adapters) implementation.
- Implementation of 6 essential design patterns (Singleton, Facade, Factory, Observer, Strategy, Decorator).
- Atomic transactions for zero data loss during checkouts.
- Multi-Role Authentication (RBAC) for Admin and Cashier roles.
- Accounts Receivable / Store Credit (*Fiado*) system.
- KatanaKit CDN Integration.
- InsForge & PostgreSQL Ready.
